CLINICAL SPECIALTIES

Integrated Outpatient Medical Services

Explore our 8 specialized healthcare divisions designed to deliver precision clinical results and empathetic care.

1. Medical Consultation

Primary medical care and thorough physician evaluations for adult acute ailments, viral illnesses, fever, and chronic lifestyle diseases.

  • Comprehensive systemic physical exam
  • Diabetes & hypertension optimization
  • Evidence-based, unrushed guidance

2. Specialist Consultation

Direct access to senior surgical, gynecological, orthopaedic, and cardiology specialists without hospital queues.

  • Surgical opinion & pre-op evaluation
  • Women's health & obstetric counsel
  • Joint, arthritis & spine consultations

3. Preventive Healthcare

Proactive wellness profiling, metabolic screening, and organ health checks designed to identify risks before symptoms appear.

  • Lipid & cardiovascular risk mapping
  • Fatty liver & metabolic audits
  • Physician lifestyle consultations

4. Clinical Procedures

Safe, sterile minor interventions performed in certified procedural suites with local anesthesia and zero unnecessary hospital stay.

  • Suturing, cyst excision & wound care
  • Orthopaedic joint injections & PRP
  • Strict autoclave sterilization standards

5. Pharmacy Services

Authenticated prescription medicine dispensary with licensed clinical pharmacists providing clear dosage guidance.

  • 100% genuine, batch-verified drugs
  • Temperature-monitored cold storage
  • Pharmacist medication review
Call Pharmacy

6. Day Care

Short-stay observation bays for IV therapy, hydration, postoperative rest, and acute symptom stabilization.

  • Continuous cardiac & vital monitoring
  • Dedicated bedside nurse attention
  • Same-day safe patient discharge

7. Health Checkups

Customized diagnostic checkup packages tailored for executives, women, senior citizens, and young adults.

  • 40+ vital blood biomarkers checked
  • Resting ECG & chest screening
  • 1-on-1 Physician consultation included

8. Diagnostic Support

Reliable pathology, haematology, biochemistry, and rapid spot testing enabling clinicians to diagnose with absolute precision.

  • Complete blood profile & biochemistry
  • Digital reporting directly to your phone
  • NABL-accredited diagnostic workflows
